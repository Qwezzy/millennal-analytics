Executive summary

Clarity is the competitive advantage.

Millennal Analytics is a premier sports data and performance consultancy based in Frankfort, Free State. We specialise in elite sports analytics and scouting data science. In modern competitive sport, fractional gains make the difference between victory and defeat. We don’t just present data — we tell the tactical and physical story behind the numbers to elevate performance, prevent injury and optimise talent acquisition.

Mission

To bridge the gap between complex sports science data and immediate, actionable pitch-side decisions. We deliver world-class athletic intelligence through custom dashboards, biometric modelling and strategic match analysis.

Vision

To be the leading sports analytics engine in South Africa and beyond, empowering teams, athletes and sporting organisations to maximise human potential and tactical superiority through data-driven clarity.

Core sports analytics services

Specially made frameworks for soccer, rugby, athletics, cricket and more.

We build analytics tailored to the demands of elite sport environments — for schools, sports departments, clubs and scouts who need usable intelligence, not more spreadsheets.

01

Player and squad performance tracking

Manage workload, track technical output and visualise spatial discipline across every session and match.

  • GPS and biometric metrics: distance, high-speed running, sprints, acceleration and heart-rate variability
  • Technical monitoring: pass completion, shots on target, expected goals (xG), tackles and turnovers
  • Positional and spatial heat maps for tactical discipline
  • Injury-risk signals from chronic-to-acute workload ratios
02

Tactical game intelligence and opponent scouting

Identify vulnerabilities, set-piece tendencies and transition patterns so coaches can adjust in real time.

  • Opponent pattern recognition from historical match data
  • Match simulation and win-probability modelling for substitution and formation decisions
  • Automated post-match breakdowns delivered to coaching staff
  • Pressing efficiency and spatial coverage views
03

Data-driven scouting and recruitment science

Find and value talent that others miss. Compare external targets against your own squad benchmarks.

  • Talent identification models for transfer and draft targets
  • Player valuation and market-efficiency calculations
  • Youth academy profiling and development trajectories
  • Readiness indicators for senior-level transition
